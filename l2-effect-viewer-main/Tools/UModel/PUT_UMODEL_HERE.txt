umodel.exe lives in this folder (UE Viewer from https://www.gildor.org/en/projects/umodel ).
The viewer reads packed effect data from inside the Unity project:

  Assets/StreamingAssets/UcEffects/Classes
  Assets/StreamingAssets/ViewerData/Meshes
  Assets/StreamingAssets/ViewerData/Textures
  Tools/UModel/umodel_64.exe

Meshes/textures are extracted on demand by UModel when an effect is shown.
Results are cached in ViewerCache/extract and Assets/ViewerEffects/Cache.
